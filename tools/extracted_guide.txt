2. Blender fundamentals Antigravity must understand
Mesh, armature, skinning. The mesh is visible geometry; the armature is a bone hierarchy. An Armature modifier and vertex groups
bind vertices to bones. A correct hierarchy does not prove good weights, and a clean neutral pose does not prove joint deformation.
Rest pose vs pose position. The rest pose is the reference configuration defined in Edit Mode. Pose Mode applies offsets.
Changing rest pose can alter existing animation behavior. Never alter it just to align the character without testing on a duplicate.
Bone roll/local axes. Bone roll rotates the local frame around the head-to-tail axis. Similar-looking bones may have different local
axes. An Euler value such as Z=-1.2 radians has no universal anatomical meaning without the bone's local frame, parent transform,
rotation mode, and rest pose.
Transform spaces. Blender distinguishes bone/channel, pose/armature, and world transforms. Every retargeting conversion must
state source and target spaces and account for the armature object's transform. Do not copy world-space landmark positions directly
into pose-bone Euler values.
IK and constraints. IK is a solver, not a guarantee of natural motion. It depends on chain, target, pole, constraints, and rest pose.
Test isolated chains before relying on IK.
Actions and glTF. Actions contain animation curves; export depends on action/NLA configuration. Matching clip names does not
prove tracks are complete or correctly bound. Re-import and play the GLB in a clean scene.
Official Blender docs should govern technical behavior. Videos are learning aids, not normative specifications.
3. Anatomy: the arm is a connected chain
A signing pose depends on shoulder girdle → upper arm → elbow → forearm → wrist → palm → finger joints. The wrist does not
teleport independently; arm joint rotations determine the hand's reachable path.

Landmark-to-rig rules

Draw landmarks/connections over source video first; verify handedness and index ordering.

Keep image-normalized landmarks separate from world landmarks; document normalization and units.

Estimate palm orientation from multiple non-collinear points; validate axes with known poses.

Calculate finger joint bends from appropriate consecutive phalanx vectors; test straight and curled synthetic poses.

Convert desired orientations into bone-local rotations relative to the actual rest pose.

Use confidence/visibility where available. Missing/all-zero hands must be marked invalid or handled explicitly.

If depth was flattened during preprocessing, cross products cannot recover lost depth motion.

5. ASL HELLO: define the sign before animating
The avatar is a communication interface, not just a rigging demo. A plausible arm wave may still be the wrong sign. Choose one
documented reference variant and keep it fixed.
Strong ASL describes one HELLO variant as starting with the side of the index finger against the forehead, then moving the hand up
and away. Other dictionaries list a distinct palm-out side-to-side greeting. Do not mix the variants.

PROJECT: SignVox — Blender rebuild from first principles. Blender: 5.2.x. Goal: reliable hand/body animation exported as GLB for
React/Three.js.

Read this guide and official references before editing assets.

Hash/inventory original FBX/GLB assets; preserve originals read-only.

Import untouched source FBX into a new .blend. Do not use modified RigWork/V2/V3/V4/V5 as the clean baseline.

Create `.agents/skills/signvox-blender-baseline/SKILL.md`, `signvox-rig-audit/SKILL.md`, `signvox-hand-landmarking/SKILL.md`,
and `signvox-gltf-validation/SKILL.md`, each with a reference checklist.

Stage 1 is read-only: report meshes, armatures, bone parents, deform flags, local axes/rolls, vertex groups, modifiers, actions,
constraints, transforms, materials, and scene objects.

Generate bone/hand report and screenshots; map Mixamo finger bones to MediaPipe landmarks.

Do not change rest pose, bone rolls, weights, constraints, actions, or front-end code during Stage 1.

After review, test isolated shoulder/elbow/wrist/finger poses on a duplicate. Repair only demonstrated defects.

Create a simple test action before authoring HELLO against one fixed reference variant.

Export only after visual approval; re-import into a clean scene and compare browser playback.

Deliver `asset_inventory.md`, `baseline_rig_audit.md`, `bone_map.csv`, `hand_landmark_map.md`, screenshots, and a pass/fail
report. Stop after read-only audit and await approval.
Do not write arbitrary Euler constants as a substitute for understanding bone axes; do not overwrite original
assets; do not batch-generate the vocabulary; do not claim sign correctness without a valid reference.
Final acceptance checklist

Original source hashes recorded and untouched.

Fresh import inspected; bone hierarchy/finger chains mapped.

Neutral, shoulder, elbow, wrist and individual-finger tests pass.

No unexplained mesh penetration, twisting, or cross-driving fingers.

Chosen ASL reference variant documented and reviewed.

GLB re-import retains the intended clip.

Browser playback matches Blender; manual controller does not stomp clip.

Every PASS has evidence; otherwise status remains FAIL/BLOCKED.
